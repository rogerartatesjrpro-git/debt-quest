Debt Quest

Turn your payoff plan into a game

Quests, XP, levels and boss fights for each loan. Everything stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Install it first

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Debt Quest from your home screen and paste your plan code there.

Your progress is saved inside the installed app, so load the plan there, not in Safari.
